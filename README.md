# 🧵 CraftPass

### Digital Market Access for India's Traditional Crafts

**CraftPass** is a market-access platform designed to help traditional artisans reach wider markets without compromising the cultural identity, authenticity, and heritage of their craft.

Developed for the **HER CAMPUS Club × IKS Innovation Challenge × Virasat Se Vikas Ideathon**.

**Theme 10 — Entrepreneurship & Livelihoods**

> *Propose a market-access or scaling model for a specific traditional craft/skill that solves a real bottleneck without diluting its cultural value.*

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Traditional artisans often have the skill and craftsmanship, but lack:

* Access to wider markets
* Digital visibility
* Direct customer connections
* A way to effectively showcase their craft and its story
* Opportunities to scale beyond local/intermediary-dependent markets

Scaling through conventional mass-production models can also risk **diluting the authenticity and cultural value** of traditional crafts.

---

## 💡 Our Solution — CraftPass

CraftPass creates a **digital bridge between artisans and markets** while keeping the artisan and their craft at the centre.

### For Artisans

* Create a digital artisan profile
* Showcase their craft, techniques, and products
* Build a digital portfolio
* Reach customers beyond their local market

### For Customers

* Discover authentic traditional crafts
* Explore artisans and their stories
* Discover crafts based on category and location
* Connect directly with artisans

### Core Idea

**Scale the market, not the craft.**

CraftPass focuses on increasing **visibility, reach, and access to opportunities** rather than forcing artisans to standardize or mass-produce their traditional work.

## 🏗️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** Next.js, React, TypeScript
* **UI:** Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui
* **Backend & Database:** Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, Storage)
* **AI:** Google Gemini API
* **Maps:** Leaflet + OpenStreetMap
* **Deployment:** Vercel
* **Version Control:** Git + GitHub

---

## 🔄 How CraftPass Works

```text
Artisan
   ↓
Digital Profile
   ↓
Craft & Portfolio
   ↓
CraftPass Discovery
   ↓
Customers / Organizations
   ↓
Market Access & Opportunities
```

The platform helps artisans **scale their reach without requiring them to scale production in ways that compromise traditional methods.**
